# THE ARISE ARC — Website plan

## Approved direction and iteration decisions

Continue the existing dependency-free, single-page Webdev site and its five-destination navigation. Rework interaction design around the user's “fine wine” concept: a calm first glance followed by deeper craft as visitors move, scroll, touch and explore. Preserve the supplied FRONTCOVER.png as the visual source of truth; keep using the high-fidelity local WebP derivative without changing the artwork. Use the cover as a physical product, not as interchangeable decoration. Retain About, Support, Privacy, Terms, the one-place owner-editable checkout URL and honest email fallback. No account, database, payment processing, invented checkout, testimonials, credentials, or unsupported author/product claims are needed. The page remains light; auto-publish is off and no publication is requested.

## Design system — layered luxury editorial

- **Design movement:** luxury editorial art direction at the intersection of fine-wine packaging, architectural product websites, premium fragrance campaigns, watchmaking and tactile still-life photography. The effect comes from restraint, layered materials and precise details—not a template or effects showcase.
- **Core principles:** (1) quiet, legible confidence at first glance; (2) discovery earned through natural input; (3) each reactive detail has an editorial purpose; (4) honest, accessible and performant craft.
- **Color philosophy:** retain the cover-led mix of warm ivory, pearl, light lavender, soft purple and muted champagne gold. These colors suggest daylight, fine paper, silk and gilding; deep plum is reserved for readable type and buttons. Keep the site unmistakably light. No neon, cyberpunk, gaming aesthetic, heavy glass, loud darkness or excessive gradients.
- **Layout paradigm:** a continuous, asymmetrical editorial journey with spacious “chapters,” not a collection of card panels. Keep the product object as the visual spine: framed in the opening composition, meeting the reader in the guide passage, and returning with greater presence at the purchase moment. Keep the five requested destinations, sticky navigation, white space and obvious routes intact.
- **Signature elements:** (1) the actual cover as a dimensional object with a visible page edge; (2) one fine gilded filament/arc whose position or completion carries meaning; (3) a directional light field that subtly answers input and warms at the purchase moment. Do not scatter decorative particles or unsupported objects.
- **Interaction philosophy:** first, explain the promise and give one obvious way into the guide. Next, invite natural cover exploration with a light instruction; pointer movement changes orientation, reflected light and shadow, while a low-impact horizontal touch gesture offers exploration on phones without taking vertical scroll away. In the recognition passage, each of six observations can be opened for one short, empathetic note—no quiz, score or saved answers. In the transformation passage, details appear only on a deliberate open/focus, without presenting the complete framework. Magnetic/tactile purchase feedback is restrained and keyboard-visible; it brings the product visually forward, never obstructs the actual CTA. Only HOME, ABOUT, SUPPORT, PRIVACY POLICY and TERMS AND CONDITIONS remain primary destinations.
- **Animation:** remove ornamental always-on loops. Use requestAnimationFrame only to batch active pointer/scroll updates. A single passive scroll listener updates currently visible sections observed by IntersectionObserver; progress drives restrained camera/depth changes and an arc completion. Stop work off-screen and on `prefers-reduced-motion`. Hover, focus, press and touch states remain useful without motion. No canvas particles, third-party libraries, scroll snapping, scroll hijacking, permanent CPU work or interaction that blocks text selection, touch scroll, navigation or checkout.
- **Typography system:** retain the local Georgia/Times display serif and system sans stack; no remote font request. Use strong editorial hierarchy, generous leading, restrained uppercase chapter captions, short sentences and fluid clamp sizing.
- **Brand essence:** a practical guide for people tired of repeatedly restarting, helping them build a direction they can return to without hype. Personality: composed, candid, crafted.
- **Brand voice:** concise, experienced and emotionally intelligent; recognize the tension before suggesting a way forward; never shout, imply urgency, or sound like generic AI marketing. Existing voice examples: “You know what matters. Staying with it is the hard part.” / “Build a rhythm you can return to.”
- **Wordmark & mark:** keep the custom THE / ARISE ARC wordmark and its fine open-arc/gilt-point motif; use it consistently with the supplied cover.
- **Signature brand color:** soft lilac `#d8c9e6`, grounded by warm white and muted champagne gold.

## Experience and content behavior

Open with a cinematic, light atmospheric composition, dimensional cover, an explicit but quiet exploration cue, and concise copy about stopping the cycle of starting over. Layer the story so the promise is immediately understood, then movement, natural scrolling and deliberate interaction reveal small details. The recognition passage makes six lived behaviors—endless scrolling, abandoned projects, planning instead of acting, waiting for motivation, switching tasks, and doing the easiest thing rather than the important thing—feel seen through optional micro-reveals, never a quiz. Move the visual mood from scattered to clear without darkening the page. Use the product's repeated, unchanged cover as the journey's anchor; subtly change its perspective and scale at visible story stages, then bring it forward in a warm, refined purchase composition. Do not expose a full table of contents, chapter summaries or the complete method. Explain the problem, cost of inconsistency, value of discipline, intended reader, product purpose and realistic reasons to own the complete guide. Keep copy concise; invent no fake urgency, scarcity, reviews, achievements, credentials or founder story. Support stays `mailto:dewifystores@gmail.com`; Privacy and Terms must describe only implemented site behavior and an actual third-party seller/checkout flow if one is later connected. One `site-config.js` value controls the purchase URL, with the tested support-email fallback when blank.

## Implementation structure

- `index.html`: semantic one-page document, five-destination navigation, cinematic hero, accessible recognition/reading interactions, story sections, legal/support content, metadata and local resource links.
- `styles.css`: light luxury editorial art direction, physical cover geometry, pointer-light/proximity states, subtle journey transitions, responsive layouts, touch affordances and complete reduced-motion/focus styles.
- `app.js`: progressive enhancement for configured purchase links, mobile navigation, bounded pointer/cover lighting, safe touch exploration, recognition/reading reveals, visible-section scroll choreography, magnetic/purchase states and current-section indication. Event-driven only.
- `site-config.js`: the single owner-editable checkout URL; blank by default until the owner supplies a real checkout URL.
- `cover.webp`: site-local high-fidelity derivative of the supplied official 1024×1536 cover; keep the original identity and artwork intact.
- `favicon.svg`: original open-arc/gilt-point brand mark.
- `manus-routes.json`: declaration for the single `/` route.
- `README.md` and `TODO.md`: owner instructions and source-traceable outcome criteria.

Keep this as a dependency-free static site on the existing configured port 3000; no backend, external libraries, analytics, form or new collection of personal data. Serve the supplied cover and all browser assets on same-origin relative URLs. Preserve the current metadata and legal/support functionality while implementing the new interaction direction.
